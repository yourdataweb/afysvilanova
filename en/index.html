About us

A team, not just another office

Afys Vilanova is an accountancy practice on Plaça de Soler i Gustems, in the centre of Vilanova. It works with individuals, freelancers and small businesses who want their paperwork in order and someone who answers when they call.

What clients value most is clarity: knowing what has to be filed, when, and what happens when the rules change. Each person in the team has their own specialism, and queries do not sit waiting in a drawer.

They have looked after the same businesses and families for years, which means they know the history and do not start from scratch every year.

What clients say, again and again, is the same thing: they answer, and you can count on them.
